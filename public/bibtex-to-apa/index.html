papertools
URL → APA BibTeX → APA Markdown compactor

BibTeX → APA citation

Paste one or many BibTeX entries and get APA 7 references with proper italics, like Google Scholar's “Cite” box — then copy them as rich text, Markdown, LaTeX or plain text. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Converts as you type. Ctrl/⌘+Enter converts immediately.

      Notes
      
      
        
    • Sentence case is a heuristic: every word after the first is lowercased except the first word after a colon, question mark or other sentence break, words with an internal capital (GPT-4, LLMs, iPhone), and single capital letters. Proper nouns cannot be detected without a dictionary, so protect them with braces in BibTeX: title={Introducing {Claude} 3}. Journal names keep their capitalisation in APA 7 style (and are sentence-cased in Scholar style, mirroring Google Scholar).
      • 
        
    • Author list style: APA 7 lists up to 20 authors and, for 21 or more, the first 19 followed by “…” and the last author. Google Scholar still uses the APA 6 rule: up to seven authors are listed in full; with eight or more you get the first six, “...”, and the last. Scholar style also omits DOIs and URLs and prints “(Year, Month)” for conference papers.
      • 
        
    • Braces protect case. {BERT} or {{Deep Learning}} in a BibTeX title is never re-cased; the braces themselves are removed from the output. Escaped braces (\{, \}) are printed literally.
      • 
        
    • LaTeX is decoded to Unicode: accents such as \"{o}, \'e, \c{c}, {\L}, \ss; -- and --- to en and em dashes; ``quotes'' to typographic quotes; ~ to a non-breaking space; \emph{}, \textit{} and \url{} are unwrapped. The standard month macros (jan…dec) and @string definitions are expanded.
      • 
        
    • Entry types: @article, @inproceedings/@conference, @book, @incollection/@inbook, @phdthesis, @mastersthesis, @techreport, @misc (arXiv preprints are detected from eprint/archivePrefix or an arXiv URL), @online, @software, @dataset, @patent. Unknown types are formatted generically with a warning.
      • 
        
    • Sharing: “Share link” puts your BibTeX into the URL fragment (#bib=…). Fragments are never sent to any server, so the link is still fully private; it is only available for inputs under 8 000 characters.
      • 
        
    • Found a bug or a wrongly formatted reference? Please open an issue on GitHub (link in the footer) and include the BibTeX entry.
      • 
      
    
      
    Everything runs locally in your browser: nothing you paste leaves this page. Options are remembered in your browser's local storage.
